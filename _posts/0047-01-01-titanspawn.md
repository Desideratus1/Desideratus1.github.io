As I'm sure you're already well aware, Janthir is the second worst place on the planet[^fn-worst]. Unsurprisingly, a lot transpired between the last mention of Janthir in this journal and this entry, so much so that instead of going on my lengthy narratives for what happened, I'm going to give you a quick version for one tale and a medium version for the other. First, the quick version of the first series of events.

Honestly I'm not sure where to start with this one. On some level Ebonhawke being the shithole it is spawned a family that just could not help but randomly fall into evil or whatever and some of them turned into a cult of something. They call themselves the Circle of Conquest and while I don't particularly understand their goals or broadly speaking anything about them, they're the usual sort of fucked up fanatics hellbent on their secret evil goals and generally terrorizing the rest of the planet[^fn-hunch]. The problem with the Circle is that they're the best in the world at fucking up. I first heard of them when I heard that they had attacked a bunch of Ministry officials and hangers-on in Doric and whisked away a few hostages to Janthir where a big crowd of the evil cultists were gathering. I didn't really care for the Ministry or the hostages, and by and large I considered the whole affair to be beneath me. But I was bored so whatever.

Anyway the short rendition of this tale is that the Circle was trying to use and abuse Bloodstone and infuse a bunch of black[^fn-obsidian] armor with the souls of those sacrificed to the stones. As I'm sure you're also well aware bloodstone has a habit of doing whatever the hell it wants to do and your opinion on the matter is more often than not disregarded. So despite some absolutely catastrophic failures on the ends of my "compatriots"[^fn-compatriots] the cultists fucked up even worse and the spirits in the armor snapped and obliterated the vast multitude of cultists stupid enough to wear the shit. But the leader of the group whose name I don't remember was an accomplished necromancer, and instead of being killed off she survived[^fn-encounter] and retreated north to the Barrens where. We, though the compatriots kept fucking up, eventually killed her. The Circle is still around though, probably seething and crying and plenty of other things at their resounding failure to do anything correctly.

So with that out of the way, Janthir was also a host to a bunch of Titans and their spawn were running around. Naturally because people are stupid they looked around at all the dead titanspawn laying around and tried to figure out a way to make money off of it. And a lot like bloodstone this inevitably backfired, but not quite in the way the bloodstone armor did. You see, the titan organs were grilled or ground or juiced or something into what was essentially an elixir of magical strength. A hell of a lot of magic mixed in with raw Torment such that imbibing the resulting concoction would supercharge your magic, though obviously coming at the cost of having all of that awful Torment running through your system.

What led me to discovering this illicit trade was actually something completely different; Shortt had approached me saying that he suspected his supplier[^fn-purchased], an employee of some waypoint Krewe who was dealing him stuff on the side, had been found out and their next meeting was likely a trap to also catch Shortt. I and Erebus went with him to the meeting point and instead of a trap it was a message directly to Shortt essentially saying "The screams you are hearing from this recording is me killing your contact. Stay out of my business".

Not for nothing, a message like that would usually work. Nobody wants to go up against someone gleefully murdering someone for a recording. The fatal flaw in this plan was that I was now involved, and upon hearing the death screams I decided that the person making these recordings was solidly high-tier evil and had to be dealt with. After some investigating it became apparent who the Krewe was, and who had made the recordings. As far as I can remember they're more of the investor types than the creating things kind of Krewes, but they had their hands in just about everything which made them rich and I imagine capable of a lot of corporate violence to achieve their goals. So I figured I would just bust into the corporate office and lay waste to it, but Erebus and Shortt thought of a better plan to just kidnap the Krewe lead with some well placed waypoint tampering. With the Krewe's leader suddenly missing the Krewe has since devolved into infighting over who the successor is, more or less neutering them for the next few years at least.

So that more or less solved that problem but we had also learned around the same time that a lot of money had gone to the Krewe via an apothecary in Lion's Arch. Me being the good, kind, supportive, citizen of the world that I am, I decided to meet with this apothecary with the intent of seeing her money (which we came into possession of when we nabbed the Krewe leader) which would hopefully mean she wouldn't come after us. Which, this takes us to the real meat of the last few weeks. Yea I know, nearly a thousand words to get here but whatever[^fn-simplified].

Now we come to the part where the Titans are gone, but their organs are being trafficked from the battles in Janthir off to the far corners of the world. The apothecary in question was using these Titan organs to whip up some extremely dangerous and admittedly effective tonics. The magical empowerment is off the charts like I stated above, but you run a very real and dangerous risk of turning into a Titan if you take too much. Naturally, because people are stupid, they immediately started pushing for people to take too much to make deathmatches in Lion's Arch more interesting. I tracked down one of the prime distributors of the organs in Lion's Arch and confronted him at one of these deathmatches. When it became apparent that one of the participants was quite literally turning into a demon before our eyes, I knew we had a very fucking bad problem on our hands. Through various threats, attempts at diplomacy, and sleuthing, I was able to discover that at least a major pusher in the arena of this bullshit was a Ward Guy[^fn-guy] named Waxwing. From the earliest intelligence gathering, mostly through asking Zoott, it was determined that this guy is actually pretty damn dangerous. Astral Ward by default are usually dangerous anyway, but apparently he was a level above the rest. Not only that, he could seemingly teleport long distances at will. Great.

And naturally when you're in this kind of realm the clientele were their own kind of dangerous too. One, named Andrin, is definitely some kind of fucked up necromancer that eats people and maybe also memories. The others that I can make actual notes of were a renegade Flame shaman and an Inquest operative. Actually fixing this problem (i.e. killing Waxwing and his immediate subordinates, as I ruled out diplomacy immediately) was sort of easy and sort of impossible. You see, Waxwing regularly met with his clientele for auctions or poetry readings or whatever in the depths of Drizzlewood in some shitty house on a small plateau. So all of my targets were gathering in one place on a semi-regular basis where most of their defenses would definitely not be. The problem then, is how the hell I could take of potentially fifteen or twenty people, of whom most are probably dangerous in their own right, all of whom will be working together to take me out.

The answer to this question was using the same kind of potion that I was trying to stop the production of. With its power I would bring down judgement onto the meeting, all of its participants, and clean the whole thing up with no survivors but myself and those friendlies stupid enough to join me[^fn-erebus] and wise enough to stay out of the way[^fn-erebus-two] after I took the potion.

So, to set the scene both myself and Erebus met some ways away from the meeting site in the dense thickets of Drizzlewood to discuss the plan. A few hours prior, a cache of material which included two vials of the concoction carrying the juiced[^fn-juiced] titan material and two stun grenades was put there. Two Charr in armor that was heavily enchanted were guarding the rickety shack where the meeting was taking place. We crossed a small stream, at which point I threw both grenades at the front of the door to the building and downed the vial. Immediately after, Erebus took his shotgun to the two stunned guards to eliminate them. Upon imbibing the potion, my magical capabilities felt stronger by an order of magnitude, and I descended into the most intense feelings of hate and rage that I had ever experienced. You can imagine it as an intense tick in the back of your head, overriding your thoughts and forcing you to hate. Everyone and everything; Every person, every building, every rock and blade of grass was mine to do with as I pleased, and deserved every misery I was about to inflict. The shack was pulled off of its foundations, crushed, and flung into the trees in a brief but violent tornado.

I couldn't really tell who was in it at that point. I know of an Inquest operative that was crushed, and Andrin was somewhere in the mess. The Flame shaman too, and most likely Waxwing but these things I could only barely make out in the debris and combat. Somehow the Flame shaman had survived the experience, and turned on me with his magical fire. I was able to hunker down, using my skills in weaving to bring out all of the elements to my disposal and protect myself from the worst of it. This also set the entirety of the forest surrounding us on fire. Then, I think, he was shot by Erebus before trying to run. At that point I controlled gravity itself, and he was crushed and annihilated. The concussive blast of this event threw myself and everyone at least a few feet back, and once I was on my feet I was turning on everything left standing which at this point was mostly trees in various stages of burning.

I could feel that Torment digging deeper, embedding itself into my soul as it tried to take over. Now that I was no longer in danger, I turned my attention to it and fought back. It was, by that point, starting to morph me into something else. That ticking in the back of my mind grew so loud and so forceful that I could scarcely understand my own thoughts. It was a white noise of sorts, drowning in foreign thoughts. Soon I would be gone, and the only thing that would remain of me would be the twisting mass that wore my skin and saw through my eyes.

Naturally, in these moments where thoughts that are not my own are creeping in, whether they are illusions or forceful attempts at takeover, I've found that a certain amount of crippling pain does wonders for focusing the mind and defending against it. As I had done several times before, I pulled out the orichalcum dagger on my hip, and drove it into my right leg to the hilt. Coincidentally this seems to have been the right choice for reasons other than just focusing the mind, as the black mass of building torment spewed from the wound. Before it could attempt to reenter through the same place, I grabbed the mass and crushed it with my teeth[^fn-teeth]. Once more I was then in complete control of my body, and called for Erebus to carry me out of the burning forest as I sure as hell could not walk with a knife in my leg. Apparently Andrin also survived the experience, somehow, though thankfully he proved to be non-hostile even though we just tried to kill him. Whatever, I guess that's fine.

That more or less ends this section. Waxwing and most of his buyers are dead, though the flow of Titan organs into Lion's Arch and no doubt other areas of the planet will continue, albeit slowed down thanks to my effort. Maybe that means less pointless deaths on our end, maybe not. At least I tried, more than I could say otherwise. The Circle of Conquest is still running around fucking things up but is still somewhat dangerous, so that problem isn't solved yet. I do still have a remaining potion with which I don't think I'm going to be using any time soon. I came too close, went too far, and nearly died by my own hand because of it. The remaining potion will be used when my death is already assured and maybe I don't care about my soul or something.
