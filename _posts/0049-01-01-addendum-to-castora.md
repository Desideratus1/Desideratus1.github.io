This won't be very long and very narrative-y but I didn't want to leave this as an unfinished piece. Suffice it to say, once Zoott was secured and I was off the island I more or less washed my hands of the whole affair and went back to how things were. Naturally though I got pulled back in when it came to my attention that Cilla was reaching into other Tyrias to kidnap people and try to force them into fighting the Ward. I didn't really have any stake in the fight between the Inquest and the Ward after my job there was done, but seeing Cilla do some egregious bullshit like **OPENING SOME PORTALS TO ALTERNATE TYRIAS TO KIDNAP PEOPLE FOR HER WAR EFFORT** was enough to "grab my attention". Grab my attention in this case meaning to make me earth-shatteringly apoplectic because why in the god damn fuck did she ever think that was even close to something resembling the shadow of a hint of a good idea? Still mad about that one. Of all the brainless shit to pull off, pissing off a whole other reality is up there.

I tried to negotiate with the Ward as much as I could to help me deal with the problem easily, though that quickly fell apart when they wouldn't budge on anything ever at all ever any time ever[^fn-ward]. Eventually I had to agree to some non-disclosure non-fuck-with-my-shit agreements to be able to take Cilla down without having to assault the caves with all of her Inquest friends being there to provide support. It went well in the sense that Cilla was dead by the end of it, but poor in the sense that some catastrophic fuckups on our end led Cilla to being shot in the head, and then - still living - beg for her life and I held her down.

I'm not going to talk more about that. Ever. What I am going to talk about next is that there were still Inquest stuck in that cave. While everyone was happy to see them dead for the usual reasons[^fn-inquest], I knew them a bit better and decided I should do something and get them out of that cave so they don't all die there in some lightless, damp, miserable shithole slowly starving to death or eventually being hunted down in the tunnels and butchered. I had it in my mind that I could negotiate a sort of surrender of the Inquest by which they would leave the island with all of their equipment intact, while the Ward would naturally be left in control. The Inquest suffers a strategic defeat, but I save everyone I can from pointless blood and misery in the process. Negotiations with the Ward however, as before, were short. The Ward commanders were seemingly more interested in causing the Inquest casualties than they were actually getting the thing they were all supposedly fighting over. I guess on some level it was strategically smart to cause the Inquest as many casualties as possible, but were I sure that the Ward would not have come after me for making the attempt I would have shot the Ward commander in the back of the head the moment she turned away. The world would have been a better place had I done so.

So without the Ward's help I had two choices. Try to break them out myself, or... Something? Look, I was low on options at that point. Frankly, I was prepared to go back into those caves and die im a futile attempt to turn into a one man army and just kill all of the Ward on the island. Given I am one person and the Ward, while staffed by concentrated fuckup, has a lot of resources to call on, I was just prepping myself to die pointlessly on some shithole rock. But, it turns out that having friends does in fact have its uses. If you recall that krewe that worked in the Mists from the "Rant" entry, they still were on good terms with me and I used their specialties to make some small portals into the bastion, guided by my knowledge of the terrain to be where the Inquest were. And so I ferried all twenty Inquest operatives out of the caves, tossed some explosives against the walls, and collapsed the whole damn thing as I was leaving.

Not that it really slowed the Ward down, like a week later I picked up that they got through. Still, felt nice to make them have to dig through the rocks for a while. The Inquest I saved were given free reign to do as they pleased. Either they could disappear back into Rata Sum and abandon the war, or head back to rejoin. I didn't bother keeping track of who went where, though to my knowledge some disappeared, some rejoined, and some yet have found something else[^fn-not].

So, there we have it. The Ward was ultimately successful, the Inquest suffered a strategic defeat and casualties were not as horrendous on either side as they could have been, thank the Alchemy. Some twenty souls, Zoott among them, were alive thanks to my efforts. I suppose in the end that is all I could have ever reasonably asked for.

[^fn-ward]: There has to be some kind of Mists-induced brain diseases affecting them, as there is no other explanation for this organization being so condescendingly assured of themselves while at the same time being just awful at their jobs.
[^fn-inquest]: Inquest naturally not being well liked by anyone ever for what I figure are obvious reasons at this point.
[^fn-not]: With the understanding that if they went back in to the war I would not be saving them a second time. I had fulfilled by own moral obligations by saving them once and had no obligations to do so a second time.
